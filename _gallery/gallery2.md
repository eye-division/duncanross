title: New Work
description_markdown: >-
  I have spent many years developing my practice in ceramics, with a particular
  focus on the possibilities of terra sigillata. My work has always been rooted
  in a close, tactile understanding of clay and surface—an approach that, over
  time, has brought a quiet but meaningful international recognition for its
  refinement, subtlety, and technical precision.


  Rather than approaching terra sigillata as a historical technique to be
  revived, I have treated it as a living process—something to be tested,
  reworked, and gradually evolved. This exploration became central to my
  practice, leading to surfaces with a particular luminosity and depth that feel
  integral to the work.


  Throughout, I have been guided by a commitment to clarity and restraint: forms
  that carry a quiet authority, and surfaces that reflect a sensitivity to clay,
  touch, and process.


  I am now stepping away from making ceramics. While my focus turns elsewhere, I
  carry forward an enduring commitment to material, texture, and making.


  A selection of works from my personal collection will be made available for
  sale. These pieces mark key moments within the practice and present a rare
  opportunity to acquire works that have been retained over many years.
