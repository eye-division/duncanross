description_markdown: >-
  &nbsp;I have spent many years developing my practice in ceramics, with a
  particular focus on the possibilities of terra-sigillata. My work has always
  been rooted in a close, tactile understanding of clay and surface - an
  approach that over time has brought international recognition.


  Throughout, I have been guided by a commitment to clarity and restraint: forms
  that carry a quiet authority, and surfaces that reflect a sensitivity to clay,
  touch, and process.


  I will be presenting an online exhibition of new work which will open on 1st
  October 2026.  The work will be available to view from 26th September.&nbsp;
